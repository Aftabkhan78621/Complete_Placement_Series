What is Cloud Computing?

Answer (50 words)

Cloud computing means using computing services like storage, servers, databases, and software over the internet instead of your own computer. It allows users to access resources anytime without managing physical hardware.

Real-life Example:
Using Google Drive to store files online instead of your laptop.

2. What is SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS?

Answer (50 words)

SaaS provides ready-to-use software, PaaS provides a platform to build applications, and IaaS provides virtual servers and infrastructure. They offer different levels of cloud services depending on user needs.

SaaS: Gmail
PaaS: Heroku
IaaS: Amazon EC2
3. Difference between CRM and ERP

Answer (50 words)

CRM focuses on managing customer relationships, sales, and support. ERP manages internal business operations such as finance, inventory, HR, and manufacturing. CRM improves customer satisfaction, while ERP improves overall business efficiency.

Real-life Example:
A shopping app uses CRM for customer support and ERP to manage stock and billing.
